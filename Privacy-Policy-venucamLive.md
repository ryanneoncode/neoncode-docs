# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-19  
**App:** Venucam Mobile Web App ("Venucam")

Venucam respects your privacy. This policy explains what data may be collected when you use the app and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

Venucam may collect:
- **Account-related information** you submit in-app (for example, username or profile fields where applicable).
- **User-generated content** such as comments submitted in the app.
- **App usage and diagnostics** (for example, crash/debug/telemetry data from platform or SDK tooling).
- **Network and device metadata** needed to provide service functionality.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Deliver app features (venue discovery, content display, comments).
- Improve performance, stability, and security.
- Moderate user content and enforce platform rules.
- Investigate abuse, fraud, or policy violations.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell personal data. We may share limited data:
- With service providers/processors that support app operations.
- When required by law, legal process, or lawful government request.
- To protect users, the app, or the public from abuse or harm.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for operational, legal, safety, and moderation purposes. Data may be removed or anonymized where appropriate.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data. No system is fully secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Children

If you believe a child has provided personal data inappropriately, contact us and we will review and take action as required.
