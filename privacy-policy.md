# Privacy Policy for Venucam Studio

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Venucam Studio ("Venucam", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use the Venucam Studio mobile application.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the Venucam Studio Android application and related backend services used by the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### a) Camera Content
- Images captured using your device camera.
- Image data uploaded to our backend to refresh venue content.

### b) Account Credentials and Identifiers
- Username and password submitted at sign-in.
- Username stored on-device in secure storage after successful login.

### c) Technical and Network Information
- Information required to connect to our services (for example, network status and request metadata such as IP address and timestamps).

## 3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

We process information as necessary to:
- Authenticate users.
- Enable image capture and upload functionality.
- Process venue refresh requests.
- Maintain, secure, and improve service reliability.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose information only:
- To our service infrastructure and processors that operate Venucam services.
- To comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable government request.
- To protect rights, security, and integrity of Venucam, users, or the public.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, and legal purposes.

- Uploaded image data is retained according to service operational requirements.
- On-device data remains until removed by app logic, user action, or app uninstall.

## 6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Venucam Studio is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may:
- Decline camera permission (core app features may not function).
- Request access, correction, or deletion of your information.
- Uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

## 9. International Data Transfers

If you access the app from outside the country where our services are hosted, your information may be transferred to and processed in other jurisdictions.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes are effective when posted at this policy URL with an updated effective date.
