# Venucam Studio Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Venucam Studio has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

## Our Standards

Venucam Studio prohibits using the app or related services to create, upload, store, share, request, promote, or distribute any content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation.

This includes, without limitation:
- Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), whether real, altered, or AI-generated for exploitative purposes.
- Grooming, sexual extortion, trafficking-related abuse, or any exploitation of minors.
- Attempts to use the platform to facilitate abuse or illegal contact involving minors.

## Enforcement Actions

If we detect or receive credible reports of CSAE-related activity, we may:
- Remove offending content.
- Suspend or permanently terminate associated accounts.
- Preserve and provide relevant information to law enforcement where legally required.

## Reporting CSAE Concerns

If you believe content or behavior on Venucam Studio involves child exploitation, report it immediately:

- Email: privacy@venucam.com
- Subject line: "Urgent CSAE Report"

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services immediately.

## Cooperation With Authorities

Venucam Studio cooperates with lawful investigations and applicable reporting obligations related to CSAE.

## Policy Updates

We may update these standards periodically. Any updates will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
